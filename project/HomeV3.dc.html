03 — HOW WE WORK

A robot is a tool, not a climate plan. We look hard at what each one fixes, what it breaks and who keeps it running.

Count the upkeep

A pilot is only half the story. We ask who charges, repairs and pays for a robot in its third season, not just its first week.

Publish the failures

Robots that stalled, sank or sat in a shed get written up too, so nobody pays twice for the same lesson.

Share the tools

Checklists, test plans and parts lists from one project are posted where the next project can reuse them.
