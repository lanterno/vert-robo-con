# 70 · Summit week: change freeze, on-call and checks

**State:** open · **Milestone:** M6 · Summit · **Labels:** infra

---

- [ ] The developer is on call each summit day, watching uptime alerts and bounces
- [ ] Check that the state switches to live and back to concluded at the right times
- [ ] Check that the join-link email reached every registrant
