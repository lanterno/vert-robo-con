# 59 · The smaller forms: sponsors, speakers, volunteers, programmes, research, concerns

**State:** open · **Milestone:** M7 · After the summit · **Labels:** frontend, backend

---

**Design:** GetInvolvedV3, ResearchV3

Each one is a queue type with its own schema and a receipt email:

- [ ] Sponsor interest
- [ ] Recommend a speaker
- [ ] Volunteer
- [ ] Register interest in the Schools Programme or the Field Skills Course
- [ ] Propose a research topic, and join the research team (while Settings has it open)
- [ ] "Suggest an edit" on each white paper section, with the section reference attached
- [ ] Report a concern: private, admin-only, never exported, with a reply in 48 hours stated on the form

_Backend and frontend parts go in separate commits (and PRs where practical); tests follow in their own commit._
