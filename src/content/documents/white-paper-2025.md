## Introduction

Field robots tend to be judged on their first season. This paper looks at what happens after it: who keeps the robot running, what that costs, how it fails and what a buyer should ask before signing.

It is written for the people who buy, run and repair robots. Numbers come from members’ own deployments wherever they could share them. [PAPER TEXT]

## Field notes

Short reports from members’ deployments: crawlers checking pipelines for methane, rovers logging water levels in peat, boats mapping a flood after a storm.
